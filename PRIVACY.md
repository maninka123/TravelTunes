# Privacy Notice

TravelTunes is designed to help you discover travel music without collecting personal data.

When you upload travel photos, they are sent directly to the AI provider you select (Google Gemini, Alibaba Qwen, or DeepSeek) to analyze the mood and visual setting of your trip. Once the analysis completes, the images are immediately discarded and are never stored on our servers. The respective AI provider processes your images according to their own terms and privacy policies.

When text-only debugging logs are enabled by the administrator, the system records technical run details: the recommended songs, the AI model names, response times, the countries you chose for music, and your energy and style slider settings.

The run log does not save your photos, your image file names, EXIF location metadata, personal image notes, or your IP address.

The application also maintains a simple daily counter of total runs grouped by country and city based on standard network routing. This counter is strictly aggregated and cannot be linked to any individual user or song search.

TravelTunes uses no cookies, no tracking pixels, and no third-party analytics scripts.
